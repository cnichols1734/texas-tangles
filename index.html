Our Story

A Baytown Tradition Since 1948

For years, Texas Tangles Salon has been the heart of Baytown’s beauty community. What began in 1948 has grown into a beloved institution, serving three generations of families with the same commitment to quality, care, and personal attention that built our reputation.

“I never really write reviews, but you can just tell these stylists give off a light. I will definitely be going back!”

Our experienced stylists combine time-tested techniques with contemporary trends to deliver exceptional results. From classic cuts to modern color techniques, we bring decades of expertise to every chair. When you sit down at Texas Tangles, you’re not just getting a haircut — you’re becoming part of a legacy.

What We Offer

Our Services

From classic cuts to the latest trends, our experienced stylists deliver personalized care with years of expertise behind every service.

Women’s Haircuts & Styling

Expert cuts tailored to your face shape, hair texture, and personal style. From bobs to layers, we craft the perfect look.

Men’s Haircuts

Classic and modern men’s cuts with attention to detail. From traditional fades to textured styles.

Children’s Haircuts

Patient, friendly service for your little ones. We make haircut time fun and stress-free for the whole family.

Hair Coloring & Highlights

Full color, partial highlights, and dimensional color services. Our specialists create rich, vibrant results every time.

Balayage

Hand-painted color for a natural, sun-kissed look. Low maintenance beauty that grows out beautifully.

Perms & Texturizing

Add body, volume, and beautiful waves or curls. Modern perm techniques for natural-looking texture.

Deep Conditioning

Restore moisture, shine, and strength to damaged or dry hair. Premium treatments for healthy, gorgeous locks.

Blowouts & Styling

Smooth, voluminous blowouts and professional styling for everyday confidence or that special occasion.

Special Occasion & Updos

Weddings, proms, galas — we create stunning updos and event styles that make your special moments unforgettable.

Curly Hair Specialist

Embrace your natural texture with specialized curly cuts and care. We understand and celebrate every curl pattern.

Hair Braiding

Beautiful braids for all occasions — from classic French braids to intricate styles that turn heads.

Hair Consultations

Not sure what you want? Our experienced stylists will listen, advise, and help you discover your perfect look.
